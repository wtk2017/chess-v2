♟︎ ChessMate

chess by text link

Play chess inside your texts.

Every move is a link you send in Messages. Your friend taps it, sees the live board, moves, and sends the link back. No app to install, no account, no server holding your game — the whole match travels inside the link.

How it works

  1. Make your move on the board.
  2. Tap Send in Messages — a link is shared to your chat.
  3. Your friend taps the link, moves, and sends theirs back. That's the whole game.

Works over iMessage, SMS, RCS, WhatsApp, email — anything that carries a link. Got a ChessMate link already? Just tap it and this page becomes your board.

That link isn't a valid game

The move list is damaged or contains an illegal move, so the board can't be trusted. Ask your opponent to re-send their last link.
